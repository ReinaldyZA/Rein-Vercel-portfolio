Why Corporate profit fell while sales grew

Sales rose 20.4% but Corporate profit fell 13.6%. Furniture is the only category losing ground, down 56.6%, and discounts are the fastest lever to fix it.

Program
RevoU Understanding Business Problems, intermediate assignment
Date
July 2026
Tools
DARCI, SMART, issue tree, OBIPR, Tableau
Deliverables
13-slide deck

The question

How can the Corporate segment grow its profit by 10% over the next two months after a 13.6% decline despite higher sales?

Context

RevoShion is a fictional retailer built on the Superstore dataset. The work starts from an existing Tableau dashboard and focuses on structuring the problem before any fix is proposed.

What I did

  1. Mapped who decides, owns, does, advises and needs to know with DARCI.
  2. Wrote the problem as one SMART sentence with the target built in.
  3. Broke the profit drop into four branches: product mix, pricing and discount, customer behaviour and cost to serve.
  4. Prioritised hypotheses, matched each to a metric, read the dashboard against them and turned the results into OBIPR recommendations.

What I found

  • Only Corporate shrank. Corporate profit fell 13.6% to $26.8K while Consumer grew 27.4% and Home Office 40.3%.
  • Only Furniture shrank. Furniture profit fell 56.6% to $3.0K while Technology and Office Supplies grew.
  • Margin is thinning. Sales grew 20.4% but profit only 14.2%.
  • Orders got smaller. Orders rose 28.3% while customers rose only 8.6%.

What I recommended

  • Reprice or delist Tables and Bookcases for Corporate buyers.
  • Cap Corporate discounts at 15%, with approval required above that.
  • Set a minimum order value and bundle low-margin items.
  • Widen the buyer base with a B2B acquisition programme.
