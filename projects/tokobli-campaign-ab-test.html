Evaluating twin-date campaigns and a product page A/B test

All three campaigns returned about Rp55 of revenue per Rp1 of discount, so a bigger budget bought a bigger result, not a better one. The new product page lifted average order value by 11%.

Program
RevoU Statistics and Spreadsheet, intermediate assignment
Date
July 2026
Tools
Google Sheets, pivot tables, SUMIFS, QUARTILE, T.TEST
Deliverables
11-sheet workbook, 18-slide deck

The question

How can TokoBli get the most revenue out of its twin-date campaigns while keeping discount spending efficient?

Context

4,217 transactions from the 10.10, 11.11 and 12.12 campaigns of 2023, with 14 columns covering product, customer, price, discount, revenue and category.

What I did

  1. Cleaned the data from 4,217 to 4,209 rows: dropped a column that was 99.5% empty, removed rows with missing values and duplicates, and fixed data types.
  2. Removed revenue outliers with the IQR method and computed descriptive statistics for quantity, discount and revenue.
  3. Compared campaigns and categories on transactions, customers, units, revenue and revenue per rupiah of discount.
  4. Ran a Welch two-sample t-test on transaction value for the old and new product pages.

What I found

  • Reach was identical. The three campaigns differed by only two transactions, 1,398 to 1,400.
  • 11.11 won on value. Rp6.81 billion in revenue, 9.5% above 10.10 and 13.3% above 12.12, with the fewest customers.
  • Discount efficiency was flat. Revenue per Rp1 of discount was 55.0, 54.4 and 54.9. Men's Fashion returned 144, Appliances only 27.
  • The new page wins. Average transaction value rose from Rp745,890 to Rp830,460, with a p-value of 2.97 × 10⁻¹⁵.

What I recommended

  • Use the 11.11 setup as the template for the next campaign.
  • Move discount budget from low-return categories such as Appliances to high-return ones such as Men's Fashion.
  • Lift basket size with bundles and minimum-spend free shipping, since a typical order has one item.
  • Roll out the new product page and keep testing after launch.
