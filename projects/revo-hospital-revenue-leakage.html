Tracking IDR 14.5 billion of uncollected hospital revenue

Self-pay patients are 13% of admissions but hold 30% of all pending revenue. The backlog is spread across all eight branches, so the fix has to be a network policy.

Program
RevoU Data Visualisation, intermediate assignment
Date
September 2026
Tools
Tableau Desktop, Tableau Public, LOD expressions, table calculations
Deliverables
Interactive dashboard, 19-slide report

The question

Which patient segments and periods should the finance lead chase first to bring pending revenue below the 10% tolerance?

Context

Revo Hospital runs eight branches across Java. Over two years it booked IDR 99 billion in net revenue, and IDR 14.5 billion of it, 14.7%, is still unpaid. That money cannot fund branch operations.

What I did

  1. Picked one user and one decision: the finance lead deciding where collections should go first. That made this an analytical dashboard rather than an operational one.
  2. Modelled five tables around admissions at one row per admission, and validated the grain so billing is never counted twice.
  3. Built eight views, each chosen for a job: a KPI scorecard, segment comparison, monthly trend, bill distribution, an LOD view, a department ranking, a running table calculation and a map.
  4. Placed all filters in a fixed right rail that applies to every sheet, and wrote each chart title as a finding.

What I found

  • Self-pay drives the gap. 35% of self-pay revenue is unpaid, against 10 to 13% for insured patients. That is about IDR 4 billion.
  • The backlog is systemic. Every branch sits between 12% and 16% pending, so no single branch fix recovers the money.
  • Two departments hold 40%. Cardiology and Oncology carry about IDR 6 billion of pending revenue, and Cardiology has the highest rate at 18%.
  • Large bills fail more often. Bills above IDR 20 million are 6% of cases but 23% of pending revenue.
  • It never fixed itself. Pending revenue stayed above the 10% target in 22 of 24 months, peaking at 23%.

What I recommended

  • Require a deposit or payment plan for self-pay bills above IDR 20 million.
  • Change payment policy at network level instead of branch by branch.
  • Offer structured instalments in Cardiology and Oncology, with financial counselling for any bill above IDR 20 million.
  • Review pending revenue monthly against the 10% target and scale the collections team with admission volume.
