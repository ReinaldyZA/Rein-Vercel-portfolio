What drives an e-commerce customer's yearly spending

Five behavioural and financial variables explain 92.7% of how much a customer spends in a year. Age stops mattering once the others are in the model.

Program
RevoU Statistics and Spreadsheet, advanced assignment
Date
July 2026
Tools
Google Sheets, Excel, CORREL, multiple regression
Deliverables
Workbook, 12-slide deck

The question

Which factors significantly affect a customer's yearly spending, and how much would a new customer likely spend?

Context

The data covers 299 TokoBli customers with six candidate drivers: age, income, tenure, average session time, promos received and bounce rate.

What I did

  1. Built a Pearson correlation matrix across all seven variables.
  2. Ran a multiple regression with all six drivers.
  3. Used stepwise elimination to drop variables that were not significant. Age went out in the first iteration.
  4. Checked multicollinearity, then used the final equation to predict spending for a new customer profile.

What I found

  • Session time matters most. Correlation of 0.914 with yearly spending. Each extra unit of session time adds about Rp6,342.
  • Promos pay back. One additional promo is associated with about Rp136,148 more spending per year.
  • Bounce rate costs money. Each extra point of bounce rate takes away about Rp49,209.
  • The new customer beats average. Predicted spending is Rp10.98 million a year, 13% above the Rp9.70 million average.

What I recommended

  • Make average session time a primary KPI and grow it with better recommendations, search and reviews.
  • Broaden promo reach with bundles and tiered vouchers based on purchase history.
  • Bring bounce rate down with faster pages and a simpler checkout.
  • Build a tenure-based loyalty programme.
